Three steps, zero effort

How it works

  1. 1

    Work like normal

    Open Daywell in a tab. It quietly counts real time at your desk, not a dumb timer.

  2. 2

    Get a friendly nudge

    After about 50 minutes of actual sitting, you get one small, doable idea. Snooze it anytime.

  3. 3

    Walk away. That's it.

    Step away and come back: the break is already logged with its real length. No buttons.
