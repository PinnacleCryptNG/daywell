Good to see you

Daywell notices how long you've really been at your desk and suggests small breaks.

No guilt, no guessing.

  • Measured, not a timer. Uses this browser's idle signal (keyboard, mouse, screen lock) so a walk you just took actually counts.
  • Breaks log themselves. Step away, come back, and the break is already there with its real length.
  • Honest numbers. Every value says where it came from. Nothing leaves this computer.

Chrome or Edge will ask to let Daywell know when you're idle. If you'd rather not, you can track manually.
